# Industrial Energy KPI Database

Turning raw 15-minute meter data from a steel plant into reliable energy
performance indicators that support energy-efficiency decisions.

**Status:** In progress.

## 1. Problem

Managing & monitoring energy consumption in an industrial plant requires a robust analysis and verification of metering data in order to propose intelligent management strategies and spot any drift in the plant's energy behaviour. This project replicates the standard process a robust energy monitoring would look like. 

## 2. System

A steel plant whose electricity consumption is metered every 15 minutes.
Measured quantities: active energy (kWh), lagging and leading reactive energy
(kVarh), lagging and leading power factor (%), CO₂ emissions (tCO₂), and the
load type (light, medium, maximum).

## 3. Data

- **Source:** UCI Machine Learning Repository — *Steel Industry Energy
  Consumption*. Raw data is not versioned in this repository; download it
  from the source and place the CSV in `data/raw/`.
- **Coverage:** year 2018, 15-minute resolution, 35,040 records.

### Assumptions

- Timestamps mark the **start** of each 15-minute interval (series runs from
  2018-01-01 00:00 to 2018-12-31 23:45). Not confirmed by the source
  documentation; impact limited to intervals at day boundaries.
- The source has no daylight saving time, so local time is used as-is.

## 4. Method

Raw CSV → quality checks → cleaning → PostgreSQL → SQL KPI queries →
interpreted results.

- Energy is aggregated by **sum**; peak demand by **max**.
- Average power over an interval: P (kW) = E (kWh) / 0.25 h.

cd industrial-energy-kpi-db
